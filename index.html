Welcome to Primate Explorer

Thanks for exploring the world of primates with us!

Primate Explorer is a fun and educational app that helps kids and curious minds learn about all 694 species of primates — from chimps and gorillas to lemurs and monkeys!

If you have any questions, feedback, or need help with the app, we're here for you.

Let's learn and protect the amazing creatures we share this planet with!

Features

Discover the fascinating world of primates — all 694 species in one beautifully designed app! From tiny lemurs to majestic gorillas, Primate Explorer is your ultimate guide to every monkey and ape species on Earth. Whether you're a curious student, wildlife lover, or lifelong learner, this app makes exploring the world of primates fun, educational, and inspiring.

  • Photos & Facts: See real images and learn fun facts for all 694 primate species
  • Habitat Explorer: Discover where each species lives across the globe
  • Smart Search: Easily find species by name, family, or location
  • Kid-Friendly Design: Simple, colorful, and fun to use
  • Conservation Matters: Learn which species are endangered and how to help

Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us.
